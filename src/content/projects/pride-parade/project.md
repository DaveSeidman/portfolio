To kick off Pride Month, <a href='https://b-reel.com/' target='_blank'>B-Reel</a> wanted to do something big, but personal at the same time. To create a personal, empathetic experience while still showcasing the users' place in a massive community, we decided to create a 'parade' of faces. We built a mobile/desktop website that streamed the user's face into a 3D float that they could customize, record, and then add to the larger "virtual parade."

::video
src: ./media/pride-parade.mp4
poster: ./media/pride-parade.png
::

We used Beyond Reality's latest JavaScript framework to detect a user's face in their webcam or mobile camera, then place it on a Pride float. We did the compositing by moving an invisible 3D head in sync with the user's head in real time. Everything from eyebrow raises to mouth positions was tracked, even unlocking easter eggs with secret facial gestures. Users could record their float and add it to the 'parade' of other floats.

The site received great traffic and average engagement times of over 5 minutes. We found that users were making lots of videos and sharing them on social media, which helped further promote the experience.
