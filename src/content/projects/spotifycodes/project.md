Spotify had just released <a href='https://www.spotifycodes.com/' target='_blank'>Spotify Codes</a>, a new way to quickly share a track, an album, or an entire playlist with people near you. Just click the camera icon in the Spotify app and point it at your friend's phone to start listening!

::video
src: ./media/spotify-codes.mp4
poster: ./media/spotify-codes.png
::

They came to me to help create the explainer website, which also gave users a way to generate customized codes by selecting some music, then picking the size, color, and format of the code. It also offered a direct sharing mechanism, even though most users would export and print the codes for stickers, banners, and billboards. You've probably seen them in the wild without knowing what they are. Next time, open the Spotify app and check it out!
