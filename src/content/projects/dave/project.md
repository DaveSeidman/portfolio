I'm a NYC-based full-stack developer specializing in XR projects. I've been working with web and 3D technologies for over 20 years. I grew up with the Commodore 64, Apple IIe, TI-89, Nintendo Virtual Boy, dot-matrix printers, and other technology that I programmed, dissected, and occasionally put back together.

My career has led me to work for startups, marketing agencies, and major brands like Uber, LG, Spotify, Google, Samsung, Verizon, and Amex. I've also worked on research projects for Meta Reality Labs and MIT’s New Lab. They’ve trusted me to find and execute solutions for the kinds of problems that can't be searched on Stack Overflow or asked of ChatGPT. My work has been featured at CES, CTIA, and aboard a Blue Origin <a href='/LivingDistance' target='_self'>rocket</a> that went into outer space.
